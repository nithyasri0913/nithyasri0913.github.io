Research

Safe Reinforcement Learning with Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability Analysis
Research Assistant, xLab, University of Pennsylvania | Sep 2025 - Present

Implemented safe reinforcement learning by combining Hamilton-Jacobi reachability analysis with PPO, achieving 40% reduction in safety violations in autonomous navigation tasks while maintaining task performance. Developed a safety Q-network using PyTorch that predicts constraint violation costs and applies safety shielding by filtering unsafe actions before execution. Validated algorithms in multi-agent autonomous vehicle simulation environments, demonstrating practical application to safety-critical systems.

Multimodal Emotion Recognition from Audio and Video
Independent Research | April 2024

Achieved multimodal emotion recognition from audio and video, improving accuracy by over 60% compared to unimodal models, by combining audio features (STFT, MFCC via Librosa) and video features (facial landmarks with OpenCV) and applying CNNs with decision-level fusion. Demonstrated robust emotion classification through a live application with real-world use in conversational agents, recommendation systems, and smart environments.

Projects

Image Captioning using ResNet-50 and LSTM with Attention Mechanism
Course Project | September 2021 - December 2021

Generated descriptive captions for 10,000+ images, measured by BLEU and CIDEr scores, by extracting features with ResNet-50 and training an attention-enhanced LSTM. Enhanced caption relevance and alignment with visual regions through hyperparameter tuning and attention visualization, demonstrating ability to handle large-scale, complex vision-language tasks.

Style Transfer using CycleGANs
Team of 2 | September 2022 - December 2022

Achieved high-quality artistic style transfer on images, measured by visually enhanced outputs, by developing and optimizing CycleGAN generator/discriminator networks with perceptual loss and instance normalization.

Collusion Detection in Auctions Using Clustering & Statistical Analysis
Course Project | January 2022 - May 2022

Detected dynamic collusion and enhanced auction fairness by identifying anomalous bidder groups and bid rotations using DBSCAN clustering, Markov Chains for sequence modeling, and Z-Score statistical analysis.
